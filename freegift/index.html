Quick answers.

Is this really free?

Yes — genuinely free. No shipping charges, no hidden fees, no subscription. This is our way of saying thank you for choosing Renewera.

Why do you need my Amazon order number?

We verify every request against a real Renewera purchase to make sure the gift reaches the customers who earned it. One gift per order.

What happens after I submit?

You'll get an email with a unique link within a few minutes. Click it, enter your shipping address, and we'll ship your gift. The link is single-use and expires in 14 days.

Do I need to leave a review?

Not required. We'd love it if you'd share an honest review on Amazon, but the gift is yours either way.

When will my gift arrive?

Once you complete the claim, orders ship within 2–3 business days. US standard shipping takes 5–7 days from there.

Will you share my information?

Never. Your info is used only to ship your gift and occasionally email you about new Renewera products. Unsubscribe anytime.
